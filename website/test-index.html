Joint-First Execution Guide

5-Day Split • Optimized for Spinal & Shoulder Safety

🛑 The Golden Rules

  • The Badminton Veto: Do not perform Lower Body workouts on days with heavy court time. If doing upper body on a court day, lift BEFORE playing.
  • The Joint Guard: Stop at Technical Failure (1-2 reps left in the tank). Never grind out reps with broken form. Let machines stabilize.
  • The Hydration Protocol: Maintain salt/lemon hydration continuously due to monsoon humidity to protect synovial joints.
