🛑 The Golden Rules

  • The Badminton Veto: No Lower Body workouts on days with heavy court time. If doing upper body on a court day, lift before playing.
  • The Joint Guard: Stop at Technical Failure (1-2 reps in the tank). Never grind reps with broken form. Let machines stabilize.
  • The Hydration Protocol: Continuous salt/lemon hydration in monsoon humidity to protect synovial joints.
🧘 Rest day - recovery is where the adaptation happens. Hydrate, stretch, and let the joints rebuild.
