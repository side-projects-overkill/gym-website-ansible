How to export

⌚ Apple Health · Apple Watch / iPhone

  1. Open the Health app.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right).
  3. Tap Export All Health Data → Export.
  4. Save export.zip to Files, then upload it here.

⌚ Google · Pixel Watch / Fitbit

  1. Go to takeout.google.com.
  2. Deselect all, then tick Fitbit (and/or Fit).
  3. Create the export and download the .zip.
  4. Upload the zip here as-is.

🧭 Any watch

  1. In your watch or Strava app, open a workout.
  2. Choose Export as GPX or TCX.
  3. Upload the file (or a zip of several).
